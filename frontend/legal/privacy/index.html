1. Introduction

Tumalink Digital Solutions Limited ("Tumafly", "we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and processing your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019, the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, and other applicable laws of Kenya.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect your personal data when you access or use our website, mobile application or any other platform operated by Tumafly (collectively, the "Sites"), or when you make a Booking through our platform.

3. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • identification information (name, date of birth, gender and nationality);

  • passport, national identification or visa information where required;

  • contact information;

  • Booking and travel itinerary information;

  • payment confirmation details (excluding full payment card details);

  • authentication credentials

  • currency preference

  • customer service correspondence;

  • technical information including IP address, browser type, operating system and device identifiers;

  • cookies and website usage information; and

  • any other information voluntarily provided by you.

Where you provide information relating to another traveller, you confirm that you are authorised to do so.

Where a booking includes a child or infant, we will process the child's personal data only to the extent necessary to provide our services and, where required by applicable law, on the basis of consent from a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If we become aware that we have collected a child's personal data without the required consent or lawful basis, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise lawfully process such information.

9. Data Security

Tumafly implements appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to safeguard personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access.

These measures include encryption of personal data in transit and, where appropriate, at rest, access controls based on the need-to-know principle, and regular reviews of our security practices.

We do not store your full payment card details. All payment card transactions are processed securely by our PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment service provider.

10. Data Retention

Personal data shall be retained only for as long as is reasonably necessary for:

  • providing our services;

  • complying with legal obligations;

  • resolving disputes;

  • establishing or defending legal claims; or

  • meeting statutory record-keeping requirements.

Thereafter, the data shall be securely deleted, anonymised or archived in accordance with applicable law.

Subject to the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be informed of the use of your personal data;

  • access your personal data;

  • object to the processing of your personal data;

  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;

  • request deletion of personal data where lawful;

  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and

  • lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

We will respond to your valid data subject rights requests within thirty (30) days of receipt. Where permitted by applicable law, we may extend this period up to a further sixty (60) days for complex requests and will notify you of the extension and the reasons for it.

Requests relating to your personal data may be submitted to privacy@tumafly.com

12. Data Breach Notification

We are committed to protecting your personal data and maintaining appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard it against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data.

Where we become aware of a personal data breach involving your personal data, we will assess the nature and impact of the breach and take appropriate steps to contain, investigate and remediate it.

Where required under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of the personal data breach without undue delay and, where feasible, within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach.

Where the personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay. Such notification will include, where appropriate:

  • a description of the nature of the personal data breach;

  • the categories of personal data affected;

  • the likely consequences of the breach;

  • the measures we have taken or propose to take to address and mitigate the effects of the breach; and

  • recommendations on any steps you can take to protect yourself from potential harm.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, we may not notify affected data subjects of a personal data breach where notification is not required by applicable law, including where appropriate security measures, such as encryption, have rendered the personal data unintelligible to unauthorized persons or where the breach is no longer likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of affected data subjects.

15. Cloudflare Turnstile

To protect the Sites and our users from spam, automated abuse, fraudulent activity, and other malicious traffic, Tumafly uses Cloudflare Turnstile in invisible mode on certain features of the Sites, including the flight search and Booking processes. Turnstile analyses technical and behavioural information to distinguish human users from automated systems without requiring users to complete traditional CAPTCHA challenges.

In connection with the provision of this service, certain technical information (such as IP address, browser characteristics, device information, and interaction data) may be processed by Cloudflare, Inc. for security, fraud prevention, and service integrity purposes. Such processing is undertaken in accordance with Cloudflare's privacy practices.

For further information on how Cloudflare processes personal data through Turnstile, please refer to the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Policy available at: https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/turnstile-privacy-policy/.

By using the Sites, you acknowledge that such processing is necessary to protect the security, integrity, and availability of Tumafly's services and is undertaken in accordance with this Privacy Notice and applicable data protection laws.
